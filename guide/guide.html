How to Take Care of Cats

Guide

Here you will find important information regarding the three cats' personalities and important facts about taking care of them.

Please check out the Resources page for important links and phone numbers.

About the cats

Miss G

Name: Miss Girl aka Miss G (She is known as Pepper at the vet!!!).

Gender: Female

Likes: Bugs/insects, Raisin’s food, food, toys

Dislikes: shy around new people, dirty litter boxes

Sweetpea

Name: Sweetpea

Gender: male

Likes: attention, treats, flipping

Dislikes: not being offered lunch, thunder (he will hide in the closet in the front, so please keep it open!)

Requirements: You must let him jump on your back whenever he asks. You may kiss him if he permits it.

Raisin

Name: Raisin

Gender: Male

Likes: Sweetpea, screaming, flirting

Dislikes: Loud noises, vacuums

Other Information

They are allowed in the cattery/catio during daylight hours

They eat breakfast between 8am and 9am and dinner around 5pm

They are allowed to have dry food and treats at the pet sitter's discretion

Please clean their litter boxes 2x a day

Feeding

All three eat wet food twice a day. They are flexible and don’t have to eat breakfast and dinner at any set times, before you leave for work and around when you get back from work will be great for them. They split a can of the food three ways. Sweetpea gets his on the plaid mat, Miss G on the newspaper mat next to him, and Raisin likes his on the dining room rug. They often do not finish their full plates. They also really enjoy dry food. Raisin and Sweetpea eat the senior dry food (not sure why Raisin only wants to eat senior dry food…) and Miss G has a diet food, but if they eat each other’s food it’s totally fine. They can have dry food a couple times a day, they may ask for it. Miss G will get INTO the dry food OR TREATS and RIP into the bags so we have to keep the bags either up high or sealed in a box.

Cattery

If you feel comfortable, you can leave the cattery open all day for them. They enter through the closet in the kitchen. I would recommend closing the cattery when you are back from work, because they love staying out there and it’s hard to get them in. If they are not coming in, they love GRASS and if you pick some grass and wave it at them enticingly they should run into the house. Although, because you are unknown to them they might run in anyway as you approach!

Litter

They have four litter boxes in total and we will put out two extra/disposable ones for them too. Please clean the ones downstairs daily. There are several litter scoops around, you can use any! Scoop litter into the gray litter genie in the bath tub (not ideal location LOL). If you have not used one before, you just have to lift the little lid and drop the litter in. Then when you close the lid, you can pull the handle and the litter will fall to the bottom and you can push the handle back.
